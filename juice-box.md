# Juice Box

> **The juice is worth the squeeze — so stay with it.**
> Do something **100 times** to learn its essence and become proficient.

A Love Ando brand. A **rebrand of Hundy** (a.k.a. OHT / "One Hundred Thousand").

## The three laws (proven across all five runs)

Full write-ups and receipts in the
case studies.

1. **Self → Other** — every run starts about Ando (ego) and resolves into other
   people (service). Books → the Ideal Reader. Covers → the guest. Videos → filming
   others. Artifacts → removing himself entirely. Coats → someone else's name on the
   back.
2. **Compression toward essence** — mastery makes the output *shorter and simpler.*
   "Second draft = first draft − 10%." 60s video → ~0.7s clips. 3-day coat → under 2
   hours.
3. **The breakthrough around 60** — the curve is nearly identical every time
   (exciting ~20 → wall ~40 → false summit ~60 → real lessons in the final stretch).
   The last 40 teach **process and systems** (batching, efficiency), not
   fundamentals — which is what frees you to move **laterally**.

## The rebrand

Same core philosophy — new wrapper. "Hundy" was a funky name; Juice Box is a fun,
poppy design brand. (Still a love for the number structure of "One Hundred
Thousand," same as [7300](/7300) — number aesthetics are a through-line in how
things get named.) The concept itself didn't change — only the branding.

## The arc of the 100

The heart of the brand is the emotional curve you move through — nearly identical
every time:

- **First ~20** — exciting.
- **~40** — you hit the wall; it's really hard to keep doing.
- **~60** — a false summit: *"oh, I've got this."* (You don't.)
- **Final stretch** — the real lessons land, and you level up as a person for
  staying with something so easy to give up on.

The whole point is **staying with something that's easy to abandon** — especially
when you think you've mastered it but haven't.

## The five proof runs

The thesis was validated by running it across totally different crafts — and the
curve held every single time. Each run is documented as story + artifacts + lessons
+ "what rep 100 had that rep 1 didn't":

1. **100 Book Reviews** — writing;
   Stephen King's *On Writing*; the Ideal Reader.
2. **100 Threded Covers** — design;
   Midjourney + face-swap; built mobile-only in Keynote.
3. **100 Short Videos** — video;
   wordless storytelling; 60s → ~0.7s clips.
4. **100 AI Artifacts** — image; a
   wordless life archive; the endgame of the Love Ando book.
5. **100 Chore Coats** — sewing; the
   STATE signature; hobbyist → manufacturer; ties into
   The Lateral.

**The discovery:** run the same experiment five separate times across writing,
design, sewing, video, and image — and the curve is nearly identical. That's what
turns Juice Box from a slogan into a validated system. See the
synthesis for how they cross-pollinate.

## Mission

Help people become tradesmen — learn a real craft by staying past the point most
quit. A **chart of the 100** (the curve mapped and explained) would be a valuable
artifact for people to apply to their own lives.
